How the numbers are made

Counting

  • The router emits one Routed event per settled swap, and a failed swap reverts and emits nothing. This page reads every one since block , straight from the chain’s public RPC.
  • Each swap is valued once: on its USDG side if it has one, otherwise on its ETH side at the router’s own current quote for 0.01 ETH, otherwise at DexScreener’s current price. Swaps with no priced side are counted but left out of volume.
  • ETH volume uses the current ETH price for every day. Volume per venue is not additive, because a two-hop swap counts under both of its venues.
  • The fee estimate is 2 bps of volume, and a referrer’s share is 10% of that. Both are estimates, not FeeDistributor balances.
  • The page ships a snapshot in data/stats.json, refreshed by a GitHub Action, so it paints instantly, then catches up from the chain and keeps polling.
